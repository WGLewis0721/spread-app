# Spread

The weekly role spread. Roles first. Hours second. Tasks last.

The approved look is on `main`. This branch adds the weekly workflow and is not the live site yet.

- Rollover copies this week into next week. The week you leave stays as history. If next week already has tasks or days, Spread asks before replacing it.
- Open a task and add only what you need: Notes, Outline, Table, or Photo. Add more than one. In Notes, a line that starts with I. or A. continues like a Word outline. Outline is the traditional numbered view. The week list stays just the title.
- Tap a spread’s color dot to pick another color.
- Week shows Sunday at the top through Monday at the bottom. Swipe sideways for another week. The arrows still work.
- Drag a spread onto a day, or tap it and then Add. The same spread can sit on several days. Its weekly total is the sum of those days. Remove a day without deleting the spread.
- Log out is in More, under appearance. It locks the device. The weeks stay on the device.
