# Creative direction and quality standard

This is the preserved Spread v7 standard, established by the user's direction and approval on 2026-10-10. The female film is the approved reference. Grady is the requested male companion with technical verification; separate creative approval is not recorded. [ASSETS.json](../ASSETS.json) owns cut IDs, casting, scene starts, provenance and current inventory; [BASELINE.json](BASELINE.json) freezes the original film inputs.

## The experience

The viewer wears many hats and feels deflated when one demand takes the week. Offer a practical ritual: name responsibilities, choose an hour or two for each, draw time boxes, give each a purpose, then add tasks. That familiar writing becomes Spread. Make the viewer imagine their own life and family; convey relief and agency through words, spacing and motion.

This is entirely motion graphics. No rendered people, hands, faces, avatars or cinematic live-action scenes. The reference's pacing and polish are useful; its branding and footage are not reusable Spread assets.

## Locked v7 script

> You wear a lot of hats. And somehow, one thing takes the whole week. Leaving too little for family. For yourself. So you pause. Write down what matters. Give each responsibility an hour or two. Draw a box. Give it a purpose. Add the tasks. That little plan becomes Spread. Responsibilities, hours, your week, your tasks. Make the hours count.

The end-card text is **Make the Hours Count**, with the official **Download on the App Store** badge. Verify actual App Store availability before public distribution. Reproduction retains the wording; a new script requires a new version and review.

## Voice performance

Hallie: conversational character, composed confidence, lively expression and a grounded ending. The user preferred Hallie's direction with the expression and assured delivery they heard in Juno. This is performance direction for one preset, not a blend or transfer of two voice identities.

Ask for bright, clear articulation, varied emphasis, distinct thoughts, natural breaths and short sentence rests. Avoid soft/sultry/whispered delivery, monotonous runs and loud or abrasive performance. Preserve the selected identity and engine. The “So” should feel like an invitation to see a useful method; its accepted brief beat is documented in [female-v7-edit.json](../evidence/female-v7-edit.json).

Historical requests, including the actual successful audition and pivot prompt, are in [voice-recipes.json](voice-recipes.json). The WAV sources are the reproducible reference; new synthesis is variable even with the same prompt.

## Picture and motion

Use the existing [design tokens and role data](../src/design.tsx): warm paper, dark ink, restrained graphite, Spread blue and the existing responsibility colors. Use the bundled Inter font and approved paper texture. Paper lettering uses the original Georgia/Times New Roman/system-serif stack: a different OS may choose a different fallback. The preserved MP4 is the visual reference; the original Windows environment reproduced it byte for byte.

One focal object per beat. The opening can feel compressed; planning opens up room. Use smooth, purposeful frame-based motion and the existing easing. The signature transition preserves the identity of each paper block as it becomes a digital responsibility row. Text, boxes and exact interface states belong in code or verified captures. New illustrations must remain clearly illustrative and must not imply unimplemented features.

The [reference gallery](REFERENCE.md) captures four actual approved frames. For detailed changes, compare the full film around the affected transition; a still cannot prove motion quality.

## Acceptance standard

Every row must pass in the [review sheet](templates/REVIEW.md): story clarity, voice expression, human cadence, pronunciation, timing, paper/UI continuity, product accuracy, visual hierarchy, intelligible mix, end-card readability and technical integrity. A successful build, correct transcription or unclipped waveform alone cannot establish creative acceptance. Record the reviewer's decision and actual file hash so later work cannot silently replace the accepted take.
