## 2. The hinge failure was a harness error (corrects the PR #127 repair note)

Artifacts `13337464895` (run 41852591875) and `13347556086` (run 41860698620) both contain `session.json`, `hinge-closed.json` and `orientation-closed-portrait.json`, and no startup-failure files:

- the app opened (startup 20.5 s);
- `agent-device fold closed` **succeeded** (hinge 0°, LCD native panel 466 × 678 pt);
- `agent-device orientation portrait` **failed**: `UNSUPPORTED_OPERATION ... cannot rotate in a scoped simulator set` (`reason: unsupported-device-scope`).

The workflow passed `--ios-simulator-device-set`. The pinned `agent-device@0.21.36` help text says a foldable iPhone simulator "refuses every rotation in a scoped simulator set", and its source throws when `simulatorSetPath` is set, which only that flag (or `AGENT_DEVICE_IOS_SIMULATOR_DEVICE_SET`) sets. **No pose or rotation assertion has ever completed**, so earlier runs say nothing about folded, half-open or unfolded behavior.
